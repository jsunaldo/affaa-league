Step 1

Put it on your home screen

It's a real app — no App Store, no download. Add it once and it opens straight from your phone. No more digging through Dropbox or Google Drive.

📱 iPhone Safari

Tap Share (the box with the ↑), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.

🤖 Android Chrome

Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Step 2

Always land on the newest version

Trades, scores, keepers and new features drop all season long. To make sure you're seeing the latest:

🔄 Close it all the way, then reopen

Swipe the app fully off your recent-apps list before you reopen it from your home screen. Do that and you'll always get the freshest scores, trades, and tools.
